situation: |-
  An agent that convinces in a demo is not yet an agent a business unit will trust with work. The difference is rarely the model. It is whether results can be measured, whether failures become visible, and whether the system plugs into where the work already happens.
approach:
  - title: Scoping the task
    description: We break the task down until it is clear what the agent decides, what it proposes, and what stays with people.
    deliverable: Task boundaries with decision and escalation limits
  - title: Architecture and build
    description: We build along your domain. Tools, data access, context, and control flow tailored rather than assembled from a kit.
    deliverable: Working agent system in your target environment
  - title: Quality metrics and evaluation
    description: Without a measure there is no improvement. We define what a good result is and check it automatically on every change.
    deliverable: Evaluation suite with domain-specific quality metrics
  - title: Observability and delivery
    description: Logging, cost transparency, and delivery paths, so the system can be operated rather than only demonstrated.
    deliverable: Observability, cost attribution, and a delivery pipeline
outcomes:
  - Agent system tailored to your domain and your systems
  - Domain-specific quality metrics and automated evaluation on every change
  - Traceability across decisions, cost, and failure cases
  - A delivery path into the landscape you already run
  - Documented handover to your team
duration: Depends on the scope of the task and the number of systems to connect. We keep the first step deliberately small.
involvement:
  - The business unit as sponsor and reviewer
  - Engineering and operations for integration and handover
format:
  - Iterative delivery with regular, usable interim results
  - Built in the target environment rather than a sandbox beside it
  - Handover to your team as part of the engagement
entryPoint: A scoping workshop that establishes task boundaries and effort.
faq:
  - q: 'Do you build on existing frameworks or from scratch?'
    a: 'Where established building blocks fit, we use them, and we build custom where the task demands it. We give reasons for that call rather than treating it as a matter of faith.'
  - q: 'What happens after handover if something breaks?'
    a: 'Handover includes documentation and tests so your team can act on it. Beyond that, ongoing support or operations by us is available, but never a condition.'
