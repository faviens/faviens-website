track: discovery
bluf: Where AI, agentic AI, and analytics create value.
order: 1
lang: en
topics:
  - Process and opportunity mapping
  - Feasibility assessment
  - Risk and guardrails
  - Impact sizing
situation: |-
  Every organisation has a list of ideas. What is missing is the line between what sounds good and what pays back and can actually be built today. Without that line, the most visible proposals get funded rather than the most effective ones.
approach:
  - title: Process and opportunity mapping
    description: We walk the processes where time, quality, or cycle time is lost together with the business units, and capture candidates where the work actually happens.
    deliverable: Candidate list anchored to real process steps
  - title: Feasibility assessment
    description: Every candidate is tested against data availability, system access, and technical maturity. What cannot be built today is marked as such rather than quietly carried forward.
    deliverable: Feasibility verdict per candidate with stated prerequisites
  - title: Risk and guardrails
    description: Where personal data, customer contact, or regulated processes are involved, we establish early which controls a case needs to be approvable at all.
    deliverable: Risk classification and required guardrails per case
  - title: Impact and prioritisation
    description: We size impact and effort in the same unit, so the ordering becomes something you can argue about on evidence rather than taste.
    deliverable: Prioritised backlog with a stated reason for each rank
outcomes:
  - Prioritised use-case backlog with impact and effort estimates
  - Feasibility verdict per candidate, including the cases we advise against
  - Risk classification and the guardrails a case needs to get approved
  - A clear recommendation on where to start and why
duration: Scoped to fit, from a single process to several business units.
involvement:
  - Business units that own the process day to day
  - IT and data owners for feasibility and system access
  - Data protection or compliance as soon as regulated processes are in scope
format:
  - Workshops per business unit rather than one large round
  - Review of real working steps and systems
  - On site in Switzerland or remote
entryPoint: A first conversation to establish scope and the units involved.
faq:
  - q: 'What if no viable use case is left at the end?'
    a: 'Then that is the result, and it is a useful one. A reasoned no saves you a project that would have surfaced two quarters later. We say so early, and we say why.'
  - q: 'Do we need a data strategy or clean data first?'
    a: 'No. The state of your data is part of the assessment, not a precondition for it. A use case that founders on the data is exactly what this surfaces.'
---

We map your processes and identify opportunities across three tracks, agentic AI, classical AI, and analytics, and pressure-test them against feasibility, risk, and expected impact. You leave with a prioritised backlog of concrete use cases and a clear picture of where to start.
